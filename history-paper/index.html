II · The story

Read the lesson

Look at this skull. This month it was days from crossing an auction block in Paris — then France's national museum stepped in and saved it. This is Dubreuillosaurus, and this is the only skeleton of its kind ever found.

A village mayor named André Dubreuil discovered it at Conteville, in Normandy, back in 1994. A carnivore — a theropod, related to the megalosaurs — a hundred and sixty-seven million years old. Its skull is one of the most complete theropod skulls ever found in Europe.

And here's the part that gets me. Normandy's other dinosaur fossils were destroyed in the bombing raids of 1944. This is one of the last dinosaur traces the region has left.

It survived a hundred and sixty-seven million years, a world war, and the auction house — and now it belongs to everyone.
