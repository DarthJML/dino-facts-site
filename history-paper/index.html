II · The story

Read the lesson

This skull was days from crossing an auction block in Paris. In November twenty twenty-six, France's national museum stepped in and saved it. This is Dubreuillosaurus, the only skeleton of its kind ever found. A village mayor named Andre Dubrayul discovered it at Conteville, in Normandy, in nineteen ninety-four. A carnivore, a theropod, related to the megalosaurs, a hundred and sixty-seven million years old, with one of the most complete theropod skulls ever found in Europe. And here's the part that gets me. Many of Normandy's dinosaur fossils, including Caen's famous collection, were destroyed in the bombing raids of nineteen forty-four. This is one of the last dinosaur traces the region has left. It survived a world war and the auction house, and now it belongs to everyone.
